# Teymur Aghakishiyev


 Teymur Aghakishiyev has over 19 years of experience in the banking sector. Throughout his career, he has held several senior leadership positions in product development, digital payments, strategic management, and treasury. Over the past five years, he has served as **Deputy Chairman of the Management Board** and **Chief Financial Officer**.
 


 In addition to planning and managing the financial and operational activities of the business, he has led key initiatives in digital transformation and customer experience enhancement. He has been the author and executor of innovative projects aimed at developing bank cards, electronic payments, and P2P technologies.
 


 His major achievements include the establishment of the **ExpressPay** payment system, the introduction of an exclusive money transfer mechanism enabling transfers to any bank card in the country via **Zolotaya Korona**, and the implementation of digital innovations in cooperation with **VISA** and **MasterCard**.
 


 Prior to this appointment, Mr. Aghakishiyev served as **Deputy Chairman of the Management Board** and **Chief Financial Officer** at **Expressbank OJSC**.
 


 Since April 2025, he has been serving as the **Chief Operating Officer (COO)** of AccessBank CJSC, focusing on the development of payments, card business, customer experience, digital transformation, and product innovations.
 


 Mr. Aghakishiyev holds a Bachelor's degree from the *Azerbaijan Technical University* and an MBA from the *Financial University under the Government of the Russian Federation*. He speaks Azerbaijani, Russian, and English.

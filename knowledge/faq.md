# AccessBank — Frequently Asked Questions

## About AccessBank
AccessBank Azerbaijan is a commercial bank licensed by the Central Bank of Azerbaijan. It serves individual customers and businesses with deposits, loans, card products, money transfers, and digital banking through its branch network (39+ branches nationwide) and digital channels.

## Working hours and contact
- Hotline: 151 (within Azerbaijan)
- International: (+994 12) 377 00 19
- Card services hotline: (+994 12) 310 14 37 or 194
- WhatsApp: (+994 55) 440 01 51
- Email: office@accessbank.az
- Head office: 3 Tbilisi Avenue, Baku, AZ1065

Branch operating hours vary by location. Most branches operate during standard business hours on weekdays. Use the call center or the Service Networks page to check the hours of a specific branch.

## Digital channels
- myAccess mobile app — Android and iOS — for cards, deposits, transfers, payments, loan applications and repayments
- Internet Banking via the AccessBank website
- SMS Banking (2 AZN/month) for transaction notifications
- 3D Secure: free, protects online card purchases

## Common questions

### How do I log into mobile banking?
Open the myAccess app and use the credentials you set up at registration. If you cannot log in or your OTP is not arriving, contact the call center on 151 — these issues are routed to the Digital Banking team. Never share your password, PIN, or OTP with anyone.

### My card payment failed but the money was deducted
This is a card transaction issue. The Card Operations team can verify the transaction in the payment system. Provide the case agent with: the time of the transaction, the merchant or website name, and the approximate amount. Do NOT provide your full card number, CVV, or PIN.

### My transfer failed but the amount was debited
The Transfers & Payments team handles this. Provide: transfer date/time, recipient name and country, amount, and the channel you used (branch / app / internet banking). Do NOT share your password or OTP.

### How do I block a lost or stolen card?
Call the card services hotline immediately on (+994 12) 310 14 37 or 194. You can also block the card from the myAccess app under Card Settings.

### How do I apply for a loan?
Apply online through https://www.accessbank.az/en/requests/onlayn-kredit or in the myAccess app. You provide your full name, phone, FIN code, employment details, and desired loan amount. A representative contacts you to confirm details.

### What are the loan interest rates?
Cash loans: 9.5% – 24% annual; EAPR from 15%. See the loans page for specific products. Government employees in selected sectors may qualify for preferential rates of 10% – 17.5% annual.

### What is the deposit insurance limit?
AccessBank is a member of the Deposit Insurance Fund. Reimbursement follows the Fund's rules.

### I haven't heard back about my loan application
Contact the Loans & Applications team with your full name, phone number, and approximate application date. Do NOT share your password or any one-time codes.

### The service at the branch was poor
The Customer Service / Branch Operations team handles service complaints. Tell us which branch, the date/time, and a brief description of what happened.

## Security reminders for customers
- AccessBank staff will NEVER ask for your PIN, CVV, password, OTP, or full card number — over the phone, by SMS, by email, or in chat.
- 3D Secure protects online purchases by sending you an OTP. Do not share it with anyone.
- If you suspect fraud, call 151 or the card hotline (194) immediately.

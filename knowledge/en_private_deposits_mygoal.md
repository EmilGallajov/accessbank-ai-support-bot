## Key terms
- Minimum opening amount: 50 AZN or USD equivalent
- Deposit duration: 6 to 36 months
- Additional deposits: permitted throughout the term
  - Minimum increment: 50 AZN/USD
  - Maximum monthly addition: 100,000 AZN/USD equivalent
  - Final 3 months: limited to 10,000 AZN/USD maximum
- Interest payment: distributed at deposit maturity
- Withdrawal: at the bank during business hours

## Special features
- Rate growth: as the deposit amount increases, the interest earned increases
- Free debit card (myCard) for deposits ≥ 30,000 AZN equivalent
- SMS banking included
- Covered by the Deposit Insurance Fund
- Operations available at any of the bank's 39 branches

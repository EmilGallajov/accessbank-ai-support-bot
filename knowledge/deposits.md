# AccessBank Term Deposits

## Amounts
- Minimum initial deposit: 50 AZN or USD
- Maximum: not capped in product page
- Special offer: deposits of 30,000 AZN or equivalent USD receive a complimentary myCard debit card

## Interest rates — AZN
| Duration  | Monthly payout | End-of-term payout |
|-----------|----------------|--------------------|
| 6 months  | 8%             | 8.5%               |
| 12 months | 9.5%           | 10.25%             |
| 18 months | 10%            | 10.75%             |
| 24 months | 10%            | 10.75%             |

## Interest rates — USD
Campaign rates apply (valid until 01/09/2026): from 0.1% to 3.5% depending on term and payment method.

## Available terms
- 6, 12, 18, or 24 months
- Both AZN and USD accepted

## Interest payment schedule
- Monthly interest payments, OR
- Interest payment at maturity (typically higher rate)

## Deposit insurance
AccessBank is a member of the Deposit Insurance Fund. In case of an insured event, funds are reimbursed in accordance with the terms and conditions of the fund.

## How to open a deposit
1. Online ordering with staff callback
2. WhatsApp contact for remote assistance
3. Branch visit (39 locations nationwide)

Mobile access: myAccess app enables deposit management 24/7.

## Early withdrawal
Deposits may be withdrawn at any time during business hours at any AccessBank branch. Interest terms may be adjusted on early withdrawal.

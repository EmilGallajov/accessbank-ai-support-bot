- Phone hotline (local 151) or international number

## Working hours
Customers should refer to the call center hotline (151) for current working hours. Individual branch hours and addresses are listed at: https://www.accessbank.az/en/our-bank/service-networks/

## Complaint response timeline
Customer complaints receive responses within 15 business days, with potential extension to 30 days if additional investigation is necessary.

#### Terms of Use for the OK Card

###### Main conditions:

* For every full or partial 200 AZN spent, a monthly commission fee of 5 AZN is charged.
* The commission starts to accrue if the spent amount is not repaid by the last business day of the month.

###### Note:

If the last calendar day of the month falls on a non\-business day, the commission accrual date and the payment due date shall be deemed the next business day.

In this case, the outstanding amount arising from transactions performed during the period from the last business day of the month to the next business day shall be included in the commission calculation for the previous month.

### FAQ

How can I use the credit line?

With the OK card, you can make payments, withdraw cash, and perform other transactions at any time within the provided credit limit. Usage within the limit is completely flexible.

If I fully repay the debt by the end of the month, will there be any extra charges?

No. If you fully repay the used debt by 12:00 on the last business day of the month, no interest or service fee will be applied
  

  
*Example:*   
Suppose you used 400 AZN in January. If you repay this amount in full by January 31, 12:00 — there will be no additional charges.

What happens if I cannot repay the debt by the end of the current month?

If the debt is carried over to the next month, no interest is charged. Only a fixed monthly fee of 5 AZN per 200 AZN is applied. By paying the fee, you can extend your debt until the end of the credit line’s term.  
  

*Example:*  

For 430 AZN, a monthly fee of 15 AZN will be charged.  

After the fee is applied, you must pay it within 1 month.

How much is the commission for cash withdrawal or transfers?

For cash withdrawals and transfers to another account (financial transactions), a 2% commission of the transaction amount is charged and added to the debt.
  
  

*Example:*   

If you withdraw 500 AZN, a 10 AZN commission will be charged. The total debt will be 510 AZN.

What is the minimum monthly payment?

The minimum monthly payment consists only of the service fee.

What happens if I don’t make the payment on time?

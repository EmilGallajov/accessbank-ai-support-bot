# AccessBank Instant Access Deposit (instaccess)

An indefinite-term savings deposit with unrestricted access — deposit or withdraw at any time, partial withdrawals allowed, account stays active at zero balance.

## Interest rates — AZN
| Balance (AZN) | Annual rate |
|---|---|
| 1 – 999.99           | 0.1% |
| 1,000 – 29,999.99    | 3%   |
| 30,000 – 99,999.99   | 3.5% |
| 100,000+             | 4.5% |

## Interest rates — USD
| Balance (USD) | Annual rate |
|---|---|
| 1 – 999.99           | 0.5% |
| 1,000 – 29,999.99    | 0.5% |
| 30,000 – 99,999.99   | 0.8% |
| 100,000+             | 1.0% |

## Key terms
- Minimum initial deposit: 100 AZN or USD
- Deposit period: indefinite (no fixed term)
- Currency options: AZN or USD
- Interest frequency: monthly capitalization
- Deposit / withdrawal: any time, any amount, no penalty
- Account remains active even at zero balance
- Available at any of 39 branches during business hours

## Account opening
- Online application
- WhatsApp support
- In-branch at any AccessBank location

## Protection
- Covered by the Deposit Insurance Fund

# Awards

 From day one, AccessBank has been dedicated to delivering the highest quality banking services to the Azerbaijani community. Built on a foundation of integrity, transparency, and sound business principles, the Bank has positioned itself as a leader in providing top\-notch banking services and introducing innovative products for customers. AccessBank's reputation as a reliable and dynamic financial organization has not only resulted in high customer satisfaction, the primary goal of the bank, but also garnered international recognition as a leading financial institution.
 

###### "Recognition by ACCA"

AccessBank has been officially recognized by the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) as an Approved Employer!
This recognition reflects our strong commitment to professional development, compliance with international standards, and continuous support for enhancing our employees’ knowledge and skills.
We congratulate our team on this achievement and remain dedicated to advancing together on the path of sustainable growth! 

###### “Banking Awards – 2024”

At the “Banking Awards – 2024” ceremony organized by the Azerbaijan Banks Association (ABA), the bank was recognized as the winner in three prestigious categories:
• Corporate Social Responsibility
• Supporter of Financial Literacy
• ABA Member Implementing Measures to Improve Financial Accessibility for Small and Medium Enterprises

###### "Gender Equality Financing" award

AccessBank has been awarded the "Gender Equality Financing" award for its successful projects and initiatives in gender equality and women's empowerment.
The award was presented as part of the "Women's Economic Empowerment in the South Caucasus" project, implemented by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) based on the Women's Empowerment Principles (WEP) with the support of the Swiss Cooperation Organization.

###### “Banking Awards – 2023”

At the “Banking Awards – 2023” ceremony organized by the Azerbaijan Banks Association (ABA), the bank was recognized as the winner in four prestigious categories:

• Corporate Social Responsibility
• Supporter of Financial Literacy
• ABA Member Implementing Measures to Improve Financial Accessibility for Small and Medium Enterprises
• Participation in the Self\-Employment Program

###### “Banking Awards"

The Azerbaijan Banks Association (ABA) has announced its traditional “Banking Awards”. AccessBank won the “Most Active Financial Literacy Supporter” nomination in 2022\. The bank received the nomination for the Access2Success project it implemented last year. AccessBank implemented this project to improve the competitive business environment for women entrepreneurs. The bank’s goal was to support women who manage micro, small and medium businesses and intend to expand them.

# AccessBank myCard White (Visa debit)

The myCard White is a Visa debit card with cashback rewards and convenient digital management through the myAccess mobile app.

## Fees & pricing
- Card issuance: 15 AZN
- PIN replacement: free
- myAccess mobile app: free
- SMS banking: 2 AZN per month
- 3D Secure: free

## Cashback
Up to 20% cashback in 3 selected categories. The cashback category is selected once a month and can be changed the following month.

| Category | Rate |
|---|---|
| Public Transport (metro and bus) | 20% |
| Markets (supermarkets, bakeries, grocery stores) | 3% |
| Cafes & Restaurants (incl. food delivery) | 3% |
| Clothing & Accessories | 3% |
| Cinema | 3% |
| Pharmacy | 3% |
| Flowers | 3% |
| Veterinary services | 3% |
| Beauty & SPA | 3% |
| Sports retail | 3% |
| Amusement (theatres, concerts, parks) | 3% |

Details:
- Cashback is paid only for the selected category.
- No plan is created for cashback.
- Cashback earned in the selected category is displayed in the myAccess mobile app.

## Network
Visa (international)

## Transaction terms
- Cash withdrawals at AccessBank ATMs: no commission
- Card-to-card transfers within AccessBank: no commission
- External bank transfers: up to 1,000 AZN without commission
- Card-to-card transfer amount: unlimited
- Processing time: within 30 minutes

## How to apply
Apply online in 5 minutes. Required: full name, contact info, ID/FIN details, delivery preferences (branch pickup or Baku delivery).

## Additional features
- 24/7 customer service
- Card-to-card transfers via mobile app
- PIN customization
- Cash by Code (ATM-based fund transfers to non-cardholders)

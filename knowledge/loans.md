# AccessBank Loans (Cash, Mortgage, Deposit-secured, Lombard, Credit Card)

## 1. Cash loan (unsecured personal loan)
- Loan amounts: 500 AZN – 50,000 AZN
- Annual interest rate: 9.5% – 24%
- EAPR: from 15%
- Duration: 6 – 48 months
- Processing fee: 0 – 3%
- Repayment: annuity (equal monthly installments)

### Eligibility
- Citizenship of Azerbaijan
- Age: 21 – 65 years
- Employment tenure: minimum 6 months at current workplace
- Official salary requirement

### Required documents
- ID card
- Employment certificate
- Income documentation (may vary based on bank analysis)

### Advantages
- Quick registration process
- Minimal documentation needed
- Online repayment capability without branch visits
- Online application available

## 2. Real-estate-secured (mortgage-backed) loan
- Loan amounts: 10,000 AZN – 70,000 AZN
- Annual interest rate: starting from 18%
- EAPR: from 19.9%
- Duration: 12 – 59 months
- Processing fee: 1%
- Collateral: real estate owned by applicant or first-degree relative; loan cannot exceed 65% of property market value
- Repayment: annuity scheme

## 3. Deposit-secured loan
- Loan amounts: 300 AZN/USD minimum, up to 90% of deposit value
- Interest: for AZN loans = deposit rate + 6%; for USD loans = deposit rate + 3%
- FIFD range: 3.1% – 18.73%
- Duration: 3 – 36 months
- Processing fee: 1% of loan amount
- Requirements: minimum age 18; active deposit at AccessBank

## 4. Lombard (gold-secured) loan
- Loan amounts: 200 AZN – 100,000 AZN
- Annual interest rate: starting from 18%
- EAPR: minimum 18.85%
- Duration: 3 – 48 months
- Commission: 1% – 5% (minimum 10 AZN)
- Collateral: gold jewelry
- Repayment: annuity OR flexible schedule (monthly interest payments only)

### Eligibility
- Citizenship of Azerbaijan
- Age: 21 – 70 years
- ID card only (no income documentation required)

### Advantages
- No official income documentation needed
- Fastest approval process among loan types
- Ideal for urgent cash needs

## 5. myCard credit card
- Credit limit: up to 10,000 AZN
- Annual interest rate: 24% – 26%
- EAPR: 24% – 26%
- Grace period: up to 50 days per payment
- Fixed duration: 36 months
- Commission: 0%
- Cash withdrawal fees: 2% – 3% depending on ATM type

### Requirements
- Minimum age: 25 years
- ID card
- Official salary requirement

### Features
- Cash-back program (AccessQazanc)
- Online account tracking via myAccess app

## Application process (online)
1. Access online application form on bank website or myAccess mobile app
2. Provide personal info: full name, phone, FIN code
3. Specify employment details and workplace
4. State official salary amount
5. Indicate desired loan amount and term
6. Agree to credit bureau data usage
7. Bank representatives contact the applicant to confirm details

## Special conditions for government employees
Government employees (Tax, Customs, Education, Healthcare, Prosecutor's Office, Oil/Gas sectors) receive preferential rates:
- Annual interest: 10% – 17.5%
- FIFD: minimum 14.64%
- Commission: 1% – 4%
- Terms: 6 – 48 months

## Repayment methods
- Online payment via website
- Mobile app (myAccess)
- Payment terminals
- Post offices
- Bank branches
- Early repayment permitted

## Loan calculator
Online tool at https://www.accessbank.az/en/kredit-kalkulyatoru lets borrowers estimate monthly payments by entering loan amount, duration, and interest rate.

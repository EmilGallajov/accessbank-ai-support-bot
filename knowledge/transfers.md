# AccessBank Money Transfers

AccessBank offers multiple money transfer systems to help customers send funds to family and loved ones domestically and internationally.

## AccessTransfer
- Coverage: wide branch network covering most of the Republic of Azerbaijan
- Speed: transfers completed within 1 minute
- Details: domestic transfer service

## Western Union
- Global reach: more than 510,000 service points in 200 countries
- Speed: transfers processed within 5 minutes
- Advantage: competitive commission rates

## Zolotaya Korona
- Destination countries: Russia, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Turkey, and other nations
- Speed: transfers completed within 5 minutes
- Benefit: low transfer fees

## Common transfer issues
If a customer reports a failed transfer, deducted amount, or delayed payment, route the case to the Transfers & Payments department. The team will investigate via the bank's payment system logs and either credit back the amount or confirm successful delivery.

## How to send a transfer
Customers can send transfers through:
- Any AccessBank branch
- myAccess mobile app
- Internet Banking on the website

## Required documents to send
- ID card (passport or Azerbaijan ID)
- Recipient's name and country
- For larger amounts, source-of-funds documentation may be requested

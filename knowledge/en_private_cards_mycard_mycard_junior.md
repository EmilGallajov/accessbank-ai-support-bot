# AccessBank myCard Junior (debit card for children)

## Age range
For children between 7 and 17 years old.

## Parental controls
- Parents can manage their children's online payment limits
- Set cash withdrawal limits from the card
- Real-time transaction notifications keep parents informed

## Fees & pricing
| Service | Cost |
|---|---|
| Card issuance | 20 AZN |
| PIN replacement | free |
| myAccess app | free |
| SMS banking (monthly) | 2 AZN |
| 3D Secure | free |
| ATM withdrawals, transfers, replenishments | up to 300 AZN commission-free |

## Cashback
Up to 20% cashback in 3 selected categories monthly (same as myCard White):
- Public Transport (metro/bus): 20%
- Markets, Cafes & Restaurants, Clothing, Cinema, Pharmacy, Flowers, Veterinary, Beauty & SPA, Sports, Amusement: 3% each

## Customization
PIN code can be replaced with any memorable code, free of charge.

## How to apply
Order online in 5 minutes via the card application form. Requires:
- Parent's identification
- Child's ID details
- Delivery preferences (branch pickup or Baku delivery)

## Additional services
- Concierge service
- Travel insurance
- Lounge Key (airport business lounge access for ~$32 USD per visit)

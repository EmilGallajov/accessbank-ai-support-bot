### Services

#### 24/7 Personalized Banking Assistance

By acquiring the VIP package, you will have a personal representative from the bank available to you 24/7 through various communication channels. A personal representative will also provide extended service in the VIP Lounge of the Bank's Head Office

#### Online VIP Concierge

The concierge service includes the following:
 * Restaurant reservations
* Ticket booking (theater, cinema, etc.)
* Recommendations about restaurants and places to visit
* Driver booking for transfers
* Ordering of flower bouquets, gifts, etc.

#### Earn more

Maximize your earnings with exclusive discounts on our products. As a Private Banking member, you'll benefit from lowered interest rates on loans and credit cards, along with extra interest on your deposits and other savings products.

#### Travel

Through your personal bank representative, you can book air tickets or tour packages for yourself, family, and friends to any country. Just let us know your desired destination and travel dates, and we'll present you with multiple offers to choose from.

#### References and Extracts

Members of the Private Banking have the opportunity to receive an unlimited number of references from all their accounts and request letters and extracts for embassies or other authorities

#### Insurance

You will have access to the following types of insurance for yourself, your family, and loved ones:
 * Medical insurance
* Travel insurance
* Property insurance

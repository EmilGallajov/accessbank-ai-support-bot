## Benefits of a Term Deposit

#### Open your deposit account online

You no longer need to visit a branch — simply log in to the myAccess app and open your Term Deposit account online

#### Earn high interest income

By placing your funds in an AccessBank Term Deposit, you can benefit from higher interest earnings

#### Receive your interest monthly

Depending on your preference, you can receive your interest income either monthly or at the end of the deposit term

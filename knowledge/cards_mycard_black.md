# AccessBank myCard Black

## Overview
The myCard Black is AccessBank's premium debit card offering exclusive VIP benefits and comfortable privileges for cardholders seeking enhanced banking experiences.

## Card Features
- Card Types: Available as Visa
- PIN Customization: replace the PIN with any memorable code of your choice (free of charge)
- Mobile Management: card-to-card transfers available through the myAccess mobile app
- 24/7 Assistant Service: round-the-clock support for cardholder inquiries

## Benefits & Perks
- Insurance: flight delay + baggage insurance + health insurance
- Free access to airport Lounge halls (6 times per year)
- Cash withdrawal limits managed 24/7 via myAccess app
- Free access to Visa or MasterCard concierge services

## Fees & Pricing
- Card annual fee: 200 AZN
- PIN change: 1 AZN
- SMS banking (monthly): 2 AZN
- 3D Secure: free
- myAccess app: free
- Concierge service: free

## Cashback program
Up to 20% cashback in 3 selected categories (changed monthly).
- Public Transport: 20%
- Markets, Cafes, Restaurants, Clothing, Cinema, Pharmacy, Flowers, Pet Services, Beauty/SPA, Sports, Amusement: 3% each
- The cashback category is selected once a month and can be changed the following month.

## Transaction fees
- ATM withdrawals at AccessBank ATMs: commission-free (up to 2,000 AZN)
- Card-to-Card transfers: free for salary cards; 0.5% (min 1 AZN) for debit cards; 1% (min) for credit cards
- Cash by Code: transfer fee 1 AZN - 1% (max); withdrawal fee 0%

## Lounge Key Service
- Access to approximately 1,000 business lounges worldwide
- 6 visits annually free; subsequent visits $32 each
- Available at major international airports

## Travel insurance
Covers: flight delays/cancellations, baggage loss/delay, personal property damage, trip cancellation (Lux tier), medical expenses, travel accidents.

## How to apply
1. Select card type and currency
2. Provide personal information (name, phone, email)
3. Submit ID verification details
4. Choose delivery method (branch or in-Baku delivery)
5. Process completes in ~5 minutes

Delivery options: in-branch pickup, or Baku city delivery with specific time slots.

## Supporting services
- myAccess app: mobile banking platform for managing operations
- SMS banking: account status notifications for all transactions
- 3D Secure: free fraud protection for online purchases
- Cash by Code: secure ATM-based fund transfers to non-cardholders

## Restrictions
- Card delivery available only within Baku city (otherwise branch pickup is required)
- Travel insurance valid only when trip paid with myCard
- Insurance certificate available for visa applications
